Why a video is useful
A revised-ETA briefing helps delivery support explain the actual delay, reason and next step for the affected order.
The video is a review summary; the full approved source record remains authoritative.

Why the workflow complexity is justified
Only strictly later delivery estimates on active shipments qualify; unchanged estimates and delivered shipments skip. The source row carries the complete approved delay record; the workflow does not require invented child records. The persistent request fingerprint prevents duplicate completed revisions. Saved job and file checkpoints let interrupted work resume without blindly submitting another paid render or uploading another file. Revision rechecks and receipt readback protect the source writeback. One handoff asset per approved source revision. Changed business cells require an explicit new revision; reuse the completed asset for an unchanged source.
