Why the workflow complexity is justified
Only active at/below-threshold SKUs without an open replenishment request qualify; duplicates or missing buyer ownership go to review. Typed item rows join the source by parent_id and revision; their required fields preserve the authoritative detail. The persistent request fingerprint prevents duplicate completed revisions. Saved job and file checkpoints let interrupted work resume without blindly submitting another paid render or uploading another file. Revision rechecks and receipt readback protect the source writeback. One handoff asset per approved source revision. Changed business cells require an explicit new revision; reuse the completed asset for an unchanged source.
