Days 31–60: publication use-case audit

All 30 need a functional redesign before being presented as complete business automations. The existing renderer and deterministic calculations are useful building blocks. More nodes or longer notes alone do not establish a complete use case. Publication acceptance remains n8n’s decision.

This is a redesign decision matrix. These proposed integrations have not been implemented or tested by this audit. The earlier rendering results remain evidence for the current renderer only.

Required functional changes

  • A named user with a specific recurring problem and a reason a brief video helps.
  • A configured production trigger and an implemented source acquisition path, not only hardcoded samples.
  • A domain-specific eligibility/approval branch and at least one meaningful zero-work or review path.
  • Enrichment/calculation from real source records that materially affects the result.
  • Persistent state keyed by source identity, revision and destination, with the accepted render job saved before waiting.
  • A delivered business artifact and recorded outcome; retry delivery from the saved completed job instead of rendering again.
  • Manual Docker evidence for success, duplicates, changed inputs, no-work/review, and a failed-delivery resume.
  • Setup and submission prose accurately describes all required integrations and the actual limits; it does not promise acceptance.
