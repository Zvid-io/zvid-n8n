The blog post is published. The video version is still on the to-do list.

For content teams, turning every article into a short video creates another production queue: write a script, find visuals, add narration, and assemble the edit.

We built an n8n workflow with Zvid to handle that recurring production work:

  • Take the latest article from your blog feed.
  • Turn it into a short, narrated video summary.
  • Keep your branding and captions consistent.
  • Produce a finished video for your team to review and share.

It gives an existing article another format without starting each edit from a blank timeline.

Editorial judgment stays with you. Check the source text, the summary, and the visuals before publishing. A shortened script can miss context, so the original article remains the reference.

The workflow checks the render cost before making the video. Rendering uses Zvid credits, with separate script and voice costs. An optional preview lets you inspect the video draft before spending render credits.
