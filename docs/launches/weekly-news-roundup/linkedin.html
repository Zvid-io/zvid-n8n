A weekly roundup needs a repeatable production process, not just a list of links.

For niche publishers and marketing teams, collecting the week's stories is only the start. Someone still has to turn them into a video your audience can follow.

We built an n8n workflow with Zvid that:

  • Collects recent headlines from your chosen RSS feeds.
  • Drafts a short countdown of the main stories.
  • Adds narration, captions, and visuals in a consistent branded format.
  • Prepares a finished video for your review.

The example is a five-story roundup. You choose the topic and sources; the workflow handles the recurring video assembly.

The review still matters. Summaries draw on feed excerpts, so follow the source links and check the claims. Stock footage illustrates a story; it does not verify the event.

The workflow checks the render cost before production. Rendering uses Zvid credits, and script and voice generation can have separate charges. You can choose a preview before spending render credits.
