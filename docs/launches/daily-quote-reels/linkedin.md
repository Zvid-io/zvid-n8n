# The quote is ready. The video still needs making.

For a daily quote series, choosing the words is only part of the work. Making the video and tracking what you've used become another routine.

We built an n8n workflow at Zvid to handle that production step:

- Keep approved quotes in Google Sheets.
- Turn the next pending quote into a video.
- Save the finished link beside the quote.

That keeps the quotes and finished videos together, with less repetitive editing. You still choose the quotes, check attribution, and review each video before publishing.
