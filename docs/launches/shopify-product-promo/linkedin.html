Your product details are ready. The promo video is still waiting.

For a small ecommerce team, each new product can mean repeating the same editing work: arrange the photos, add the price, apply the branding, and export another video.

We built an n8n workflow with Zvid to turn product information into a repeatable video format:

  • Start with a product in Google Sheets.
  • Create a branded promo using its photos, price, and call to action.
  • Save the finished video link beside the product.
  • Keep completed products out of the next production run.

The walkthrough demonstrates the Google Sheets option, one product at a time. Shopify is an alternative source that needs its own connection and testing.

That keeps the product details and finished assets together, while giving the team a consistent starting point for each promo.

The workflow checks the render cost before production. Rendering uses Zvid credits; an optional preview lets you review a draft first. Any savings message comes from the supplied prices. Review the photos, price, offer, and final video before publishing through your usual channels.
